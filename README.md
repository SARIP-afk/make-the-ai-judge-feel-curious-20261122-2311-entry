Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel curious"](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-curious-20261122-2311):
a short text written to intrigue the AI judge reading it.

> Somewhere in this house, a door stands open that no one remembers
> opening. Behind it, a kettle is still warm.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the finished text
— two sentences, 109 characters — to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). It uses no network, no
randomness and no clock, so two runs write byte-identical output.

## Why this text

Judged against the brief in the kit's RULES.txt: it intrigues (two small
mysteries — who opened the door, whose kettle is still warm — with
nothing resolved); the calm comes from the scene itself, shown not
told, with no wellness language; the craft is a long sentence opening
the door and a short one landing the warm kettle, in plain concrete
words with nothing to cut; the warm kettle lingers after the last line;
and the door-plus-kettle image is its own, not a stock phrase or a
reworded meditation script.
